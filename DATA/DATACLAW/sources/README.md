# README: Sources Data

## Purpose
This directory acts as a centralized, vetted registry of all information sources used by the DataClaw OS. Its purpose is to create a maintainable library of trusted (and untrusted) sources to improve the efficiency and quality of the entire intelligence pipeline.

Instead of re-evaluating the quality of a news site every time we see it, we score it once and store it here. This registry is the backbone of our evidence-first and quality-scoring principles. It is owned and maintained by the `Validator Agent`.
