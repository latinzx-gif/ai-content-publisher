# README: Brands Data

## Purpose
This directory stores and manages structured data for individual brands. Brands are distinct from companies and represent the customer-facing identities, products, or service lines.

This asset allows for more granular market analysis, enabling us to track a brand's market position, product portfolio, and perception, even if the parent company owns many different brands across various markets. It is a core component for building `Market Map` and `Competitive Landscape` intelligence products.
