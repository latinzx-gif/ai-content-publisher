# README: Products Data

## Purpose
This directory stores and manages structured data for individual products or Stock Keeping Units (SKUs). This is one of the most granular and valuable data assets in the DataClaw OS.

This asset allows us to perform deep analysis on market trends at the feature and ingredient level. It is the primary source for identifying competitive feature sets, new product launch velocities, and emerging ingredient trends. It is essential for creating `Trend Reports` and detailed `Competitive Landscape` analyses.
