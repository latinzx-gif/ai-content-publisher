# Weekly Executive Review Template

## Purpose
A template for the weekly, high-level review meeting for HEAD-OFFICE. The goal is to maintain strategic alignment, make key decisions, and resolve blockers quickly.

**Frequency:** Every Monday
**Attendees:** HEAD-OFFICE Director, Business Unit Leads
**Duration:** 45 Minutes

---

## Meeting Agenda

### **1. Standing Items (15 mins)**

- **Review `HEAD_OFFICE_DASHBOARD.md` (5 mins)**
    - Each business unit lead gives a 60-second update on their status, key metric, and goal progress.
    - Any status changed to 🟡 At Risk or 🔴 Off Track? Why?

- **Review `STRATEGIC_PRIORITIES.md` Alignment (5 mins)**
    - Does the work completed last week align with the P1-P4 priority stack?
    - Is the team's focus for the upcoming week aligned with the `RESOURCE_ALLOCATION.md` framework?

- **Review Action Items from Last Week (5 mins)**
    - Quickly review the status of all previous action items.
    - Mark as Done or carry over.

### **2. Strategic Deep Dive (20 mins)**

- **Topic:** [Select ONE major risk, blocker, or opportunity identified on the dashboard. This is the core focus of the meeting.]
- **Goal:** [What is the desired outcome of this discussion? e.g., "Decide whether to pivot the DATACLAW Q3 product roadmap."]
- **Discussion Points:**
    - State the issue clearly.
    - Present the facts and data.
    - Propose 2-3 potential paths forward.
    - Debate pros and cons.
- **Decision:** [Record the final decision made.]

### **3. New Action Items & Adjourn (10 mins)**

- **Review & Assign New Action Items:**
    - Based on the discussion, clearly define any new action items.
    - Assign a single owner and a due date to each.
- **Confirm Next Week's Deep Dive Topic.**
- **Adjourn.**

---
### **Post-Meeting SOP**
- The meeting owner will update this document with the decisions and new action items and save it in `/HERMES-BRAIN/DECISIONS/YYYY-MM-DD_Weekly_Review.md`.
