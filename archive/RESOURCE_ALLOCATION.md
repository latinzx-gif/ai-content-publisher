# Resource Allocation Framework

## Purpose
To provide a clear, top-down framework for allocating time, attention, and capital across the HEAD-OFFICE portfolio, in alignment with the `STRATEGIC_PRIORITIES.md`.

## Guiding Principle
This is not a strict timesheet, but a guide for focus and decision-making. When faced with a choice, default to allocating your energy according to these priorities.

---

### **Default Resource Allocation (Quarterly Focus)**

| Priority | Business Unit | Role | Resource Allocation | Primary Focus |
|---|---|---|:---:|---|
| **P1** | **FASTWORK** | Cashflow Engine | **50%** | Systematizing client delivery; maximizing service margins; extracting reusable knowledge. |
| **P2** | **DATACLAW** | Intelligence Asset Engine | **30%** | Building and launching the first sellable data products; achieving first revenue. |
| **P3** | **INVESTMENT**| Capital Allocation Engine| **10%** | Developing the investment thesis; evaluating internal/external opportunities. |
| **P4** | **AGENT-TOWN** | Venture Engine | **10%** | Achieving MVP milestones; testing the core marketplace hypothesis with minimal resource burn. |

---

### **Operating Rules**

1.  **Time & Attention:** An individual's weekly effort should roughly mirror this allocation. For example, the majority of the week should be spent on P1 and P2 activities.
2.  **Capital:** Surplus capital generated from FASTWORK is the primary source of funding for DATACLAW and AGENT-TOWN. Investment decisions are made via the INVESTMENT OS.
3.  **Review Cadence:** This allocation will be formally reviewed each quarter during the strategic review meeting to adapt to changing market conditions and successes/failures within the portfolio.
4.  **Exceptions:** Any significant deviation from this allocation for more than one week requires explicit approval from the HEAD-OFFICE director during the `WEEKLY_EXECUTIVE_REVIEW`.
