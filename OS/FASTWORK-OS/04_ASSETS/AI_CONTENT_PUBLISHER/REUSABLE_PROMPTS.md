# Reusable Prompts

## Purpose
Capture reusable prompt architecture and prompt patterns from AI Content Legal System without copying secrets or client-private content.

## Prompt Architecture Pattern
- Use brand profile as the persistent context layer.
- Use topic and objective as the quick-generation input.
- Infer CTA, framework, and compliance rules from business type and goal.
- Offer Quick Mode for low-friction generation.
- Offer Advanced Mode for tone, framework, CTA, word count, and knowledge-source overrides.

## Reusable Prompt Inputs
- Business name
- Business type
- Target audience
- Tone
- Personality
- Brand website
- Content rules
- Topic
- Objective
- Platform
- Format
- Word count
- Knowledge sources

## Reusable Guardrails
- Avoid definitive professional advice for regulated industries.
- Add disclaimer or educational framing where appropriate.
- Avoid unsupported legal, financial, medical, or compliance claims.
- Use client-approved CTA and contact language.
- Keep output aligned with target platform and brand tone.

## Framework Defaults
- Legal, accounting, insurance, finance, and medical: PAS.
- SaaS, ecommerce, marketing agency, and startup: AIDA.
- General brands: no framework unless selected.

## Future Customization Points
- Industry prompt packs.
- Jurisdiction-specific legal disclaimers.
- Multi-language content rules.
- Platform-specific output templates.
- Client-specific forbidden claims and preferred phrases.
