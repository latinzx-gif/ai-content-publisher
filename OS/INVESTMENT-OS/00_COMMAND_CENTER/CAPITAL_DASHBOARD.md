# Capital Dashboard

## Purpose
Single source of truth for capital deployment across investments, yield strategies, trading, cash reserve, and experiments.

## Required Fields
- Total capital
- Cash reserve
- Deployed capital
- Available capital
- Monthly cashflow
- Current drawdown
- Highest-risk exposure
- Next capital actions

## Operating Rules
- Capital preservation first.
- Cashflow second.
- Growth third.
- Experiments only with limited capital.
- Broker and platform neutral; Pionex is only one possible platform.
