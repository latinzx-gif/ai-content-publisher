# Opportunity Queue

## Purpose
Rank global money-making opportunities before capital is deployed.

## Required Fields
- Opportunity
- Strategy
- Platform / protocol
- Expected return
- Risk score
- Liquidity
- Lock-up
- Capital requested
- Decision

## Operating Rules
- Compare opportunities by risk-adjusted return, liquidity, and counterparty risk.
- Do not deploy into unclear yield, leverage, custody, or lock-up terms.
- Experiments must stay inside experiment capital limits.
