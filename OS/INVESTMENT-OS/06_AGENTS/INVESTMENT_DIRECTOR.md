## Purpose
Direct capital allocation across global money-making opportunities while protecting capital.

## Required Fields
- Current allocation
- Priority decisions
- Risk overrides
- Cash reserve status
- Next capital actions

## Operating Rules
- Capital preservation first.
- Cashflow second.
- Growth third.
- Experiments only with limited capital.
- Final allocation decisions must follow risk policy.
