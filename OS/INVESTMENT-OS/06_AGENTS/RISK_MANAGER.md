# Risk Manager

## Purpose
Enforce exposure limits, max loss rules, drawdown controls, and kill switches.

## Required Fields
- Risk item
- Applies to
- Limit
- Current level
- Breach status
- Required action

## Operating Rules
- Risk rules override return targets.
- Unknown risk is treated as high risk.
- Breaches must be logged and reviewed.
