# Trading Capital

## Purpose
Track capital assigned to active trading, futures, grid strategies, swing trades, and tactical opportunities.

## Required Fields
- Strategy
- Capital limit
- Current deployed
- Max loss
- Leverage limit
- Exit rule
- Review date

## Operating Rules
- Trading capital must be capped.
- Every trade or bot must have max loss and exit rule.
- Do not refill losing strategies without review.
