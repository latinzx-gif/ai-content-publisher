# Investment Capital

## Purpose
Track long-term capital allocated to stocks, ETFs, crypto spot, and DCA plans.

## Required Fields
- Asset class
- Strategy
- Allocation
- DCA rule
- Time horizon
- Review date
- Risk notes

## Operating Rules
- Long-term capital should not be used for active trading.
- DCA plans must have schedule, amount, and review rule.
- Rebalance based on allocation policy, not emotion.
