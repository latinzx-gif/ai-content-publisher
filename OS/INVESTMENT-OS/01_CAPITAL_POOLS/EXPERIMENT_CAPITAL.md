# Experiment Capital

## Purpose
Limit capital used for new, unproven, high-risk, or experimental opportunities.

## Required Fields
- Experiment
- Thesis
- Capital limit
- Amount deployed
- Risk score
- Kill switch
- Review date

## Operating Rules
- Experiments must be small and capped.
- No experiment can be promoted without performance and risk review.
- Losses must generate lessons before more capital is deployed.
