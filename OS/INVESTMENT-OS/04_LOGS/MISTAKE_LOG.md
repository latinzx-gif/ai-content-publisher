# Mistake Log

## Purpose
Record investment, trading, allocation, and risk-management mistakes.

## Required Fields
- Date
- Mistake
- Strategy
- Cost
- Rule violated
- Fix
- Prevention rule

## Operating Rules
- Mistakes must produce a rule or process improvement.
- Do not hide losses or unclear decisions.
- Repeated mistakes trigger strategy pause.
