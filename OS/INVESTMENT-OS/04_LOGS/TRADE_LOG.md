# Trade Log

## Purpose
Track active trades, futures trades, swing trades, short-term crypto trades, and tactical opportunities.

## Required Fields
- Date
- Market
- Strategy
- Entry
- Exit
- Size
- P/L
- Lesson

## Operating Rules
- Log every active trade.
- Review losses for rule violations.
- Stop trading when loss limits are hit.
