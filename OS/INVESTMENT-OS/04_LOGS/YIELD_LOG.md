# Yield Log

## Purpose
Track income and yield generated from grid trading, staking, lending, DeFi, stablecoins, and cash equivalents.

## Required Fields
- Date
- Strategy
- Platform / protocol
- Capital deployed
- Yield earned
- APY
- Risk notes
- Action

## Operating Rules
- Track realized yield separately from projected APY.
- Review platform and liquidity risk regularly.
- Exit if yield no longer compensates for risk.
