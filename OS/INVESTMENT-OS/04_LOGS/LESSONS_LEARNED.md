# Lessons Learned

## Purpose
Capture durable lessons from investments, trades, yield strategies, mistakes, and reviews.

## Required Fields
- Date
- Lesson
- Source
- Applies to
- Rule change
- Next review

## Operating Rules
- Convert repeated lessons into risk policy or strategy rules.
- Keep lessons practical and behavior-changing.
- Remove vague lessons that do not change action.
