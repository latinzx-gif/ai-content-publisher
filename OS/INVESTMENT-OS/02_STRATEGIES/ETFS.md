# ETFs

## Purpose
Manage diversified long-term ETF exposure and DCA plans.

## Required Fields
- ETF
- Market exposure
- Allocation
- DCA amount
- DCA schedule
- Rebalance rule
- Review date

## Operating Rules
- ETFs are core allocation tools.
- Prefer low-cost, liquid, diversified exposure.
- Rebalance by policy, not market noise.
