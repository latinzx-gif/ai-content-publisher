# Stablecoin Yield

## Purpose
Track stablecoin yield opportunities across exchanges, protocols, lending, and cash-equivalent strategies.

## Required Fields
- Stablecoin
- Platform / protocol
- Yield source
- APY
- Liquidity
- Counterparty risk
- Depeg risk
- Exit rule

## Operating Rules
- Stablecoin yield still has counterparty, depeg, and platform risk.
- Prefer liquidity and transparency over highest APY.
- Size exposure by risk score.
