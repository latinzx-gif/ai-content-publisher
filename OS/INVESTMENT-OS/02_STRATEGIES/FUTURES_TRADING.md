# Futures Trading

## Purpose
Manage directional futures trades with hard risk limits.

## Required Fields
- Market
- Direction
- Thesis
- Entry
- Stop loss
- Target
- Position size
- Review result

## Operating Rules
- Every trade must have stop loss and position size.
- No revenge trading.
- Stop trading when daily or strategy loss limit is hit.
