# Futures Grid

## Purpose
Manage futures grid strategies with strict leverage and drawdown controls.

## Required Fields
- Asset pair
- Platform
- Margin
- Leverage
- Grid range
- Liquidation risk
- Max loss
- Kill switch

## Operating Rules
- Futures grid is high risk.
- Leverage must be capped before deployment.
- Liquidation risk and kill switch must be written before launch.
