# Stocks

## Purpose
Manage long-term and tactical stock investments.

## Required Fields
- Ticker
- Thesis
- Allocation
- Entry rule
- Exit rule
- Risk notes
- Review date

## Operating Rules
- Separate long-term holdings from trades.
- Position size must fit allocation and risk policy.
- Thesis must be reviewed when fundamentals change.
