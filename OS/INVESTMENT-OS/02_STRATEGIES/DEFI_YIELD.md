# DeFi Yield

## Purpose
Manage DeFi yield, staking, lending, liquidity provision, and protocol exposure.

## Required Fields
- Protocol
- Chain
- Asset
- Strategy
- APY
- Lock-up
- Smart contract risk
- Exit rule

## Operating Rules
- DeFi yield is not risk-free.
- Score smart contract, oracle, bridge, liquidity, and governance risks.
- Use limited capital until strategy is proven.
