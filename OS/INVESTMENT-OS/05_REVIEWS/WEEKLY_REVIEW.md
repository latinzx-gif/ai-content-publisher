# Weekly Review

## Purpose
Review active risks, trades, opportunities, yield, and capital actions each week.

## Required Fields
- Week
- Capital changes
- Active risks
- Trades reviewed
- Yield reviewed
- Opportunities reviewed
- Next actions

## Operating Rules
- Weekly review protects capital from drift and hidden risk.
- Pause strategies that violate risk policy.
- Move only the best risk-adjusted opportunities forward.
