# Opportunity Registry

## Purpose
Track global opportunities where capital can be deployed for investment return, yield, trading, or tactical upside.

## Required Fields
- Opportunity
- Asset class
- Strategy
- Platform / protocol
- Expected return
- Risk level
- Liquidity
- Review date

## Operating Rules
- Register opportunities before deployment.
- Compare expected return against risk score and liquidity.
- Reject opportunities with unclear custody, exit, or counterparty risk.
