# Risk Scorecard

## Purpose
Score opportunities before capital deployment.

## Required Fields
- Opportunity
- Market risk
- Liquidity risk
- Counterparty risk
- Leverage risk
- Smart contract risk
- Total score
- Decision

## Operating Rules
- Risk score must be completed before deployment.
- High-risk opportunities require smaller allocation or rejection.
- Unknown risk counts as high risk until clarified.

| Opportunity | Market | Liquidity | Counterparty | Leverage | Smart Contract | Total Score | Decision |
