# Platform Registry

## Purpose
Track platforms, exchanges, brokers, wallets, protocols, and counterparties used for capital deployment.

## Required Fields
- Platform
- Type
- Used for
- Jurisdiction
- Custody risk
- Withdrawal risk
- Notes

## Operating Rules
- Do not focus on any single broker or exchange.
- Pionex is one possible platform, not the operating system.
- Platform risk affects capital limits.
