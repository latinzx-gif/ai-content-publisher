# Pricing Model

## Purpose
To centralize the pricing for all of DataClaw's sellable intelligence products, including one-time reports and recurring subscriptions.

## Required Fields
- `Product ID`: The ID of the product from the `PRODUCT_CATALOG.md`.
- `Product Name`: The name of the product.
- `Price Type`: One-Time / Subscription.
- `Price (USD)`: The cost to the customer.
- `Price Justification`: A brief rationale for the price point.
- `Status`: Proposed / Approved / Grandfathered.

## AI Usage Rules
- AI can analyze competitor pricing data (if available in `PRICING_DATABASE`) to *suggest* a price range for a new product.
- All final pricing decisions must be made by the `DataClaw Director` based on a proposal from the `Product Manager`.
- AI can be used to A/B test pricing on a sales page, if the infrastructure is built.

## Success Metrics
- `Average Revenue Per Product`: The average revenue generated by each product in the catalog.
- `Subscription vs. One-Time Revenue Ratio`: The mix of recurring vs. non-recurring revenue.
- `Discount Rate`: The average discount given on products, which should be kept low.
