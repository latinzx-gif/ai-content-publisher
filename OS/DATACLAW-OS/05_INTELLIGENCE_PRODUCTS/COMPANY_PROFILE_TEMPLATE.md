# Company Profile Template

## Purpose
To define the standard structure for a detailed profile of a single company. This template can be used to generate on-demand reports or as a component of a larger Company Database product.

## Required Fields
- `Company Name`: The name of the company.
- `Profile Date`: The date the profile was generated.
- `Snapshot`: A table of key firmographic data.
- `Business Description`: A summary of what the company does.
- `Market Positioning`: Analysis of its position within the market taxonomy.
- `Brand Portfolio`: A list of brands owned by the company.
- `Funding History`: A summary of key funding events.
- `Recent Signals & Activity`: A list of recent news or signals related to the company.

## AI Usage Rules
- An `Analyst Agent` can be tasked to "Generate a company profile for `company_id`".
- The agent should pull all required data from the core `DATA_ASSETS` (`COMPANY_DATABASE`, `BRAND_DATABASE`, `FUNDING_DATABASE`, etc.).
- The agent can generate the narrative sections (`Business Description`, `Market Positioning`), which must then be reviewed by a human analyst.
