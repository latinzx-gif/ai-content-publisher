# Market Map Template

## Purpose
To define the standard structure and components for all "Market Map" intelligence products. This ensures consistency, quality, and scalability in production.

## Required Fields
- `Title`: The full title of the market map (e.g., "Thai Functional Beverage Market Map 2026").
- `Market Definition`: A clear, one-sentence definition of the market being mapped.
- `Axes`: The X and Y axes used for positioning (e.g., "Niche vs. Mainstream").
- `Quadrants`: The names of the four quadrants (e.g., "Leaders", "Challengers").
- `Data Points`: The items being plotted (e.g., Company Logos, Brand Names).
- `Key Takeaways`: 3-5 bullet points summarizing the most important insights from the map.
- `Disclaimer`: Standard disclaimer about the data's timeframe and sources.

## AI Usage Rules
- Once the axes and data are defined, an AI agent can be used to generate the initial plot visualization.
- An AI `Analyst Agent` can draft the "Key Takeaways" by analyzing the distribution of players across the quadrants, but a human analyst must refine them.
