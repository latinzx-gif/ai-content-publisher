# Product Database

## Purpose
To maintain a structured database of individual products (SKUs), their features, and market attributes. This is one of the most granular and valuable data assets.

## Required Fields
- `product_id`: Unique identifier for the product.
- `product_name`: The public name of the product.
- `brand_id`: The ID of the brand that owns the product.
- `product_category`: A sub-category within the brand's market (e.g., "Ready-to-Drink Coffee").
- `status`: Launched, In-Market, Discontinued.
- `key_features`: An array of key features or ingredients.
- `confidence_score`: Overall confidence in the record's accuracy (1-10).
- `last_validated`: Date the record was last checked.

## AI Usage Rules
- The `Scout Agent` and `Analyst Agent` collaborate to populate this database.
- AI can be used to scrape e-commerce sites or product announcement pages to extract product names and features, but this data must be structured and validated by an agent.
- All entries must conform to the `DATA_SCHEMA_RULES.md`.

## Success Metrics
- `SKU Growth`: Number of new, validated products added per market per month.
- `Feature-Level Insights`: Number of valuable insights generated by analyzing the `key_features` data across products.
- `Product Freshness`: % of "In-Market" products that have been validated in the last 90 days.
