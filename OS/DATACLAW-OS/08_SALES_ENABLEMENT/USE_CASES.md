# Use Cases

## Purpose
To map DataClaw's intelligence products to specific, high-value business problems or "jobs-to-be-done" for our target customers. This helps frame our sales and marketing efforts around solutions, not just data.

## Required Fields
- `Use Case`: The business problem or activity.
- `Description`: A brief explanation of the use case.
- `Target Customer`: The primary role that performs this activity.
- `Supporting Products`: The DataClaw products that address this use case.
- `Value Proposition`: The specific benefit the customer gets (e.g., "Save Time", "Reduce Risk", "Find Opportunities").

## AI Usage Rules
- AI can be used to generate a draft of a new use case by analyzing the `PRODUCT_CATALOG.md` and `TARGET_CUSTOMERS.md`.
- For marketing purposes, AI can expand on the `Value Proposition` to create more detailed benefit statements for a sales page.

## Practical Template
