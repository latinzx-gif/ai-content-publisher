# Sample Reports & Products

## Purpose
To provide a catalog of concrete, sellable intelligence product examples. This serves as a menu for the `Product Manager` to prioritize and as a showcase for the sales team.

## Required Fields
- `Product Name`: The name of the product.
- `Target Customer`: The ideal buyer.
- `Deliverables`: What the customer gets.
- `Frequency`: One-Time or Recurring.
- `Estimated Price`: The target price point.
- `Data Sources`: The core `DATA_ASSETS` used to build it.

## AI Usage Rules
- AI can be tasked to "propose a new sample product for the 'Healthcare' market" by analyzing available data assets and market files.
- The `Product Manager` must approve any sample product before it is added to the official `PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md`.
