# Demo Dataset & Lead Magnet Plan

## Purpose
To design and plan the creation of free, valuable data samples ("lead magnets") that can be used to attract target customers, demonstrate our capabilities, and generate qualified leads for our paid products.

## Required Fields
- `Lead Magnet Name`: The public-facing name of the free asset.
- `Target Customer`: The specific role this asset is designed to attract.
- `Format`: The file format of the asset (e.g., PDF, CSV).
- `Content`: A description of what the asset contains.
- `Value Proposition`: The clear benefit to the user for downloading it.
- `Call to Action (CTA)`: What we want the user to do next (e.g., buy a full report).

## AI Usage Rules
- AI agents can be tasked with generating these lead magnets automatically. For example, "Create a 1-page market snapshot for the Kids Nutrition market using approved data."
- The data used in lead magnets must be accurate but can be a limited subset of our full data asset (e.g., only top 20 companies instead of 500).

## Practical Template

### **Lead Magnet 1: Market Snapshot**

- **Lead Magnet Name:** `Thailand Functional Beverage Market Snapshot (Free PDF)`
- **Target Customer:** CPG Brand Managers, Strategy Consultants
- **Format:** 1-page PDF
- **Content:**
    - A simplified version of the full Market Map, showing only the top 10 players.
    - A list of 3 key trends shaping the market.
    - A chart showing market growth over the last 2 years.
- **Value Proposition:** "Get a free, high-level overview of the functional beverage market in minutes."
- **CTA:** "Like what you see? Purchase the full **Thai Functional Beverage Market Map** to get the complete landscape of 100+ players, detailed trend analysis, and more."

- **Lead Magnet Name:** `Top 50 Beauty & Personal Care Companies in SEA (Free CSV)`
