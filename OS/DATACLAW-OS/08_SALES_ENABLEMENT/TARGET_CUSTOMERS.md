# Target Customers

## Purpose
To define the ideal customer profiles (ICPs) for DataClaw's intelligence products. This document guides product development, marketing, and sales efforts by focusing on who we serve and what they need.

## Required Fields
- `Customer Segment`: The broad category of the customer.
- `Specific Role`: The typical job title of the buyer or user.
- `Pain Points`: The problems they face that our products can solve.
- `Buying Triggers`: The events that cause them to seek a solution.
- `Typical Use Cases`: How they would use our products.
- `Budget Range`: Their likely budget for our products.
- `Decision Maker`: The role that typically approves the purchase.

## AI Usage Rules
- AI can be used to analyze job descriptions for these roles to further refine "Pain Points."
- AI can help draft outreach emails tailored to a specific customer segment based on this document.
