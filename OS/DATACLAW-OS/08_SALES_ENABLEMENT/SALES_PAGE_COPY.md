# Sales Page Copy

## Purpose
To draft the core messaging, value proposition, and positioning statements for DataClaw. This document provides the foundational copy for the website, brochures, and email campaigns.

## Required Fields
- `Positioning Statement`: The single sentence that defines our space in the market.
- `Value Proposition`: A short paragraph explaining the core benefit we provide.
- `Why DataClaw?`: Bullet points on what makes us different.
- `Why Not Generic AI?`: A section addressing the objection "Can't I just do this with ChatGPT?".
- `Call to Action (CTA) Examples`: A bank of CTAs to use in marketing materials.

## AI Usage Rules
- AI can be used to rephrase or expand on these core messages for different audiences (e.g., "Rewrite the value proposition for an investor audience").
- AI should not be used to create the core positioning statement, which must be human-defined.

## Practical Template

---
### **1. Positioning Statement**

**DataClaw is the market intelligence platform that turns messy public data into sellable, high-confidence data assets for the Thailand and Southeast Asia markets.**

---
### **2. Value Proposition**

Tired of spending hundreds of hours on manual research only to end up with questionable data? DataClaw does the heavy lifting for you. Our hybrid team of AI agents and human analysts constantly scans, validates, and structures data on the companies, brands, products, and trends that matter. We deliver this intelligence as ready-to-use reports, maps, and databases, so you can stop wrestling with data and start making high-stakes decisions with confidence.

---
### **3. Why DataClaw? (Our Differentiators)**

*   **Evidence-First, Not Opinion-Led.** Every data point is tied to a source. Every insight is built on a foundation of facts. We tell you our confidence level so you know what's solid and what's a hypothesis.
*   **Built for SEA.** Our OS is designed from the ground up to understand the nuances of the Southeast Asian market, from local language sources to regional business structures.
*   **Data Assets, Not Just Reports.** We sell the underlying, structured data. You get a living asset you can use in your own models, not just a static PDF.
*   **Always-On Intelligence.** Our agent-based system works 24/7, so our data is fresher and our trend-spotting is faster than traditional analyst-only models.
*   **Radical Transparency.** Our `FIHU` (Fact, Insight, Hypothesis, Unknown) framework means you'll never mistake a guess for a certainty.

---
### **4. The "Why Not Generic AI?" Argument**

**Your Question:** "Can't I just use ChatGPT or another large language model for this research?"

**Our Answer:** You can, but you'll be spending your time being an *analyst*, not a *decision-maker*.

| | **Generic AI (e.g., ChatGPT)** | **DataClaw** |
|---|---|---|
| **Output** | A plausible-sounding text answer. | A structured, verifiable data asset. |
| **Source Quality** | Often hallucinates sources or uses outdated information. Cannot tell you its confidence. | Every fact is linked to a source with a **quality score**. |
| **Reproducibility**| Asking the same question twice often yields different answers. | Our process is a **repeatable pipeline**. The data is structured and consistent. |
| **True Cost** | "Free" to use, but costs hundreds of hours of your team's time to verify, structure, and analyze the output. | Paid, but delivers a finished, high-confidence product, saving you from the hidden costs of manual verification. |
| **Core Job** | Answers a prompt. | Solves a business problem. |

**In short: Use generic AI for brainstorming. Use DataClaw for decisions that have a budget.**
