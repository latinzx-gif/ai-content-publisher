# Product Roadmap

## Purpose
To track and communicate the pipeline of sellable intelligence products. This roadmap connects data assets to monetization opportunities.

## Required Fields
- `Product ID`: Unique identifier.
- `Product Name`: The name of the sellable product.
- `Product Type`: Market Map, Insight Report, Database, etc.
- `Target Market`: The primary market focus (e.g., "Kids Nutrition in SEA").
- `Status`: Idea / Scoping / In Development / For Sale / Deprecated.
- `Price Point`: The target one-time or subscription price.
- `Owner`: The Product Manager or agent responsible.

## AI Usage Rules
- AI can suggest new product ideas based on analysis of the `HYPOTHESIS_TRACKER.md` and `UNKNOWN_LIST.md` (e.g., "High number of unknowns in Market X suggests a 'Market Analysis Report' would be valuable").
- AI can generate a draft product description based on the underlying data assets.
- The `Product Manager` is responsible for all final decisions on scope, pricing, and launch.

## Success Metrics
- `Time to Market`: Average time from "Scoping" to "For Sale" for a new product.
- `Product Revenue`: Monthly revenue generated by each product.
- `Product Margin`: Revenue minus the estimated cost of research and production.
- `Product Adoption`: Number of units sold or active subscriptions per product.
