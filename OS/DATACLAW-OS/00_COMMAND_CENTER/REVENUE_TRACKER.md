# Revenue Tracker

## Purpose
To provide a clear, real-time view of all revenue generated by DataClaw's intelligence products. This is the ultimate measure of whether the OS is creating tangible value.

## Required Fields
- `Transaction ID`: Unique ID for the sale.
- `Date`: Date of the transaction.
- `Product Sold`: The name of the intelligence product.
- `Product Type`: Report, Database, Subscription, etc.
- `Price`: The amount paid by the customer.
- `Customer ID`: Identifier for the buyer.
- `Notes`: Any relevant context.

## AI Usage Rules
- This document should be automatically updated via integration with a payment processor (e.g., Stripe) where possible.
- AI can be used to analyze this data to generate insights, such as:
    - "Identify the top 3 best-selling products this quarter."
    - "Calculate the Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) for subscription products."
    - "Flag customers who have purchased multiple reports for a potential subscription upsell."
- All financial reporting must be audited by a human.

## Success Metrics
- `Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)`: Revenue from subscription products.
- `Gross Transaction Volume (GTV)`: Total value of all transactions, including one-time sales.
- `Average Revenue Per Customer (ARPC)`: The average amount spent by a customer.
- `Revenue Growth Rate (MoM)`: The month-over-month percentage increase in total revenue.
