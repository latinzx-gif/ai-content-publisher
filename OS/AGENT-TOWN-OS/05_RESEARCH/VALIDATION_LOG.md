# Validation Log

## Purpose
Record experiments, interviews, landing-page signals, creator tests, and buyer feedback.

## Required Fields
- Date
- Hypothesis
- Test
- Result
- Evidence
- Decision
- Next action

## AI Usage Rules
- Validate demand before scaling features or creator programs.
- Keep evidence concrete.
- Convert validated demand into roadmap and pack priorities.
