# Creator Requirements

## Purpose
Define what creators must provide to publish and maintain agent packs.

## Required Fields
- Requirement
- Reason
- Applies to
- Required status
- Owner
- Notes

## AI Usage Rules
- Requirements should improve buyer trust and pack quality.
- Avoid unnecessary friction for early creators.
- Separate required items from recommended improvements.
