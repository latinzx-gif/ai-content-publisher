# Pricing Strategy

## Purpose
To provide a clear and transparent framework for how Agent Packs and platform services are priced for buyers and creators.

## Operating Rules
- Simplicity is the primary goal for MVP pricing. Avoid complex calculations.
- Creator earnings must be transparent and predictable. The creator should always know exactly how much they will earn from a sale.
- All pricing changes must be communicated to creators at least 30 days in advance.

## Pricing Hypothesis (MVP)
A tiered, fixed-price model for Agent Packs is the simplest approach to validate the marketplace. It provides clarity for buyers and predictability for creators. We will allow creators to choose a tier for their pack during submission.

## Agent Pack Pricing Tiers (Creator-Selected)

| Tier | Price to Buyer | Platform Fee (20%) | Creator Payout (80%) | Intended Use Case | Example | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1: Starter** | $49 | $9.80 | $39.20 | Simple, single-task agents or powerful prompts. | "SEO Keyword Idea Generator" | Approved |

| **Premium Creator Account** | All Creators | $29/month | N/A | N/A | Draft | Phase 2. To be tested. Will include a reduced 15% commission rate. |

## Pricing Tests to Run (Post-MVP)
- **Test 1: Usage-Based Pricing for select APIs.** Offer a "pay-as-you-go" agent for a high-cost service (e.g., advanced image generation) to see if buyers prefer it over a high fixed price.
- **Test 2: Subscription Bundle.** Create a bundle of 5 "Marketing Essentials" packs for a fixed monthly fee of $99/mo to test buyer appetite for subscriptions.
