# Marketplace Model

## Purpose
To define the fundamental mechanics of how Agent Town operates as a two-sided marketplace, connecting agent-pack creators with buyers.

## Operating Rules
- All platform features must clearly serve either the Supply Side (Creators), the Demand Side (Buyers), or strengthen the Trust Model.
- The transaction model must be kept as simple and transparent as possible to encourage liquidity.
- The trust model is paramount; any feature that compromises trust must be rejected.

## Supply Side
- **Who:** "Creators" - Vetted AI experts, prompt engineers, and developers.
- **What they do:** They build, document, and submit "Agent Packs" for sale on the marketplace.
- **Why they are here:** To productize their skills, reach a broader audience, and generate recurring revenue without building their own sales and distribution platform.

## Demand Side
- **Who:** "Buyers" - Businesses, developers, and power users.
- **What they do:** They discover, purchase, and deploy Agent Packs to solve specific problems or automate workflows.
- **Why they are here:** To save time and resources by leveraging pre-built, trusted AI solutions instead of building from scratch.

## Marketplace Promise
- **For Buyers:** "The fastest way to deploy a trusted AI agent for any specialized task."
- **For Creators:** "The best platform to productize and monetize your AI expertise."

## Transaction Model (MVP)
1.  A Buyer discovers an Agent Pack in the catalog.
2.  The Buyer purchases a one-time license for the pack at a fixed price.
3.  The payment is securely processed by Agent Town via Stripe.
4.  The Buyer receives access to download and deploy the Agent Pack.
5.  Agent Town takes a commission (e.g., 20%) from the sale price.
6.  The remaining revenue (e.g., 80%) is paid out to the Creator at the end of the month.

## Trust Model
1.  **Vetted Creators:** All creators are manually vetted for expertise and identity verification during the MVP phase.
2.  **Manual Pack Review:** Every submitted pack and update is manually reviewed against the `QUALITY_STANDARD.md` before being listed.
3.  **Buyer Reviews & Ratings:** Buyers can rate and write reviews for packs they have purchased, creating a reputation system.
4.  **Transparent Pricing:** All prices and platform fees are clearly displayed at checkout. No hidden costs.
5.  **Secure Transactions:** All payments are handled through a trusted, PCI-compliant payment processor (Stripe).

## Success Metrics
- `Liquidity Rate`: The percentage of listed packs that have at least one sale per month.
- `Creator Retention`: The percentage of creators who list a second pack within 3 months of their first.
- `Buyer Repeat Purchase Rate`: The percentage of buyers who purchase more than one pack within 3 months of their first purchase.
- `Average Star Rating`: The average rating across all reviewed packs.
