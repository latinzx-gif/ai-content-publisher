# Revenue Model

## Purpose
To detail the specific streams through which Agent Town will generate revenue, ensuring the platform's financial sustainability and growth. This document distinguishes between platform revenue and creator earnings.

## Operating Rules
- The Agent Town Director owns this model. All changes must be approved by them.
- All revenue streams must be tested and validated with a small user cohort before a full-scale rollout.
- Creator payouts are considered a Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) and should be clearly separated from platform operational expenses.

## Revenue Streams

| Stream | Description | Frequency | Target Phase | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Marketplace Commission** | A percentage fee (take rate) applied to every Agent Pack sale on the marketplace. | Transactional | MVP | Agent Town Director | **Active** |
| **Premium Creator Subscription** | A recurring monthly fee for creators to access advanced features like detailed analytics, promotional tools, and a reduced commission rate. | Monthly | Phase 2 | Creator Manager | Hypothesis |
| **Platform Subscription for Buyers** | A recurring monthly fee for buyers to access a curated library of "Agent Town Essentials" packs, created and maintained by the platform itself. | Monthly | Phase 2 | Product Manager | Hypothesis |
| **Usage-Based Fees** | Fees charged for agents that require significant platform-hosted computation, API calls, or data storage, beyond a generous free tier. | Usage | Phase 3 | Product Manager | Hypothesis |
| **Enterprise Licensing** | Custom pricing for large organizations requiring bulk pack licenses, dedicated support, or private deployments. | Annual | Phase 3 | Agent Town Director | Hypothesis |

## Take Rate Strategy (Marketplace Commission)
- **Standard Rate:** 20%
- **Founding Creator Rate:** 10% (for the first 6 months to incentivize early adoption).
- **Premium Creator Rate:** 15% (for creators subscribed to the Premium Creator plan).

## Revenue Risks
- **Low Transaction Volume:** Insufficient pack sales will make the commission model unsustainable. Mitigation: Aggressively focus on creator quality and buyer acquisition.
- **Creator Disintermediation:** Successful creators may be tempted to sell their packs directly to customers to avoid the platform fee. Mitigation: Provide overwhelming value through discovery, trust, and creator tools.
- **Pricing Pressure:** Competitors or market expectations may force commission rates down, impacting profitability. Mitigation: Build a strong brand and trusted ecosystem that justifies the platform fee.
