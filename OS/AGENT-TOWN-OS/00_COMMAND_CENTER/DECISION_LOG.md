# Decision Log

## Purpose
Record strategic product, marketplace, monetization, creator, and governance decisions.

## Required Fields
- Date
- Decision
- Category
- Reason
- Owner
- Impact
- Review date

## AI Usage Rules
- Record decisions once and reference them instead of reopening them.
- Decisions should protect platform scale, asset quality, and marketplace trust.
- Pricing, governance, and revenue-share decisions require owner review.
