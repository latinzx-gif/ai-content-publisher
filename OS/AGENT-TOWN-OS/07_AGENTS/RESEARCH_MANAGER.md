# Research Manager

## Purpose
Own research for competitors, market opportunities, buyer demand, creator supply, and validation.

## Required Fields
- Research question
- Category
- Sources
- Findings
- Confidence
- Decision supported
- Next action

## AI Usage Rules
- Separate facts, insights, hypotheses, and unknowns.
- Research should drive roadmap, pack catalog, or monetization decisions.
- Avoid research that does not change action.
